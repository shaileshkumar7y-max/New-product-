# BIG BOSS KE RANDIYA — Feature Map

## Included in this frontend release

1. **House Feed** — simulated daily updates and task announcements.
2. **Contestant Board** — #05 and #08 concept contestants with local votes.
3. **Housemate Entry** — local nickname and vibe.
4. **Task Arena** — timed confidence, runway, chemistry and soundtrack challenges.
5. **Power Cards** — skip token, double points, house DJ, choice card, pause power and style captain.
6. **Secret Missions** — optional kindness, style, soundtrack and chemistry missions.
7. **After Dark Deck** — Truth, Dare and Chemistry with three non-graphic intensity levels.
8. **Eyes on the Prize** — reaction-time mini-game.
9. **Confession Room** — local-only note save and clear.
10. **Chemistry Check** — select shared comfort preferences before a round.
11. **Alliance Builder** — temporary local team and goal selection.
12. **Innerwear Runway Vote** — BLXCKED, Sydney Innerwear and Pink Lace campaign choices.
13. **Private Feedback bridge** — opens the existing local text-only 18+ feedback page.

## Future frontend ideas (no backend required)

- **Nomination Night** — local secret ballot and reveal animation.
- **Immunity Vault** — unlock a local prize card at score milestones.
- **House Theme Selector** — choose purple arena, gold lounge or after-hours mode.
- **Double Trouble Pair Round** — two people share a device and take turns selecting prompts.
- **Style Battle** — compare two campaign looks using user-chosen criteria.
- **Finale Ladder** — season progress, badges and a final winner screen.
- **Secret Diary** — local notes with a clear-all control and no auto-sharing.
- **House Soundboard** — original UI sound effects with a mute toggle.

## Hard rules

- Adults only; age check currently self-attestation, not formal verification.
- No explicit sex acts or graphic sexual content in the app prompts.
- Any physical contact or intimacy is outside the app and only by mutual, current agreement.
- Pass, pause and stop must always remain available.
- No private media uploads, recordings or public intimate content.
